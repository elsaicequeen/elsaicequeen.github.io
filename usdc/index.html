Where USDC stands

Five readings on Circle's dollar stablecoin: its share of the market, where it sits across chains, where it moved this month, how tightly it held $1, and which routes its cross-chain transfers take. The sentences and charts are computed in your browser when the page loads, from DefiLlama's public data and from CCTP burn events read off the chains themselves.

Method and sources

Market share
Daily circulating supply from DefiLlama: /stablecoincharts/all for every USD-pegged stablecoin, and the daily tokens history from /stablecoin/2 (USDC) and /stablecoin/1 (USDT). Share is the coin's supply divided by the all-USD total on the same UTC day. The year-ago comparison uses the closest day on or before the same date last year.
Chains
USDC's per-chain supply from /stablecoins, current and 30 days earlier (circulatingPrevMonth). Concentration is the Herfindahl index of chain shares; its inverse reads as "this many equal-sized chains". A change in a chain's supply nets mints, burns and bridging together.
Peg
Daily prices from /stablecoinprices (one reading per UTC day, the last 365 days). Deviation is (price − 1) × 10,000 in basis points. The 10 bps band is a working alert threshold chosen for this page, not an official one.
Cross-chain transfers
Circle's CCTP moves USDC between chains by burning it on the source chain and minting it on the destination. Each burn emits a DepositForBurn event on the TokenMessenger contract (V1 and V2). bake/cctp.mjs reads those events with eth_getLogs from free public RPC nodes, keeps USDC burns only, and decodes the amount (6 decimals) and the destination domain, which it names with Circle's domain table. Event layouts and addresses come from Circle's contract source and developer docs. A few decoded transfers per chain are checked against the same transaction's USDC burn. Fast means a V2 transfer with a finality threshold of 1000 or less. The page reads the baked file; it makes no RPC calls itself.
Fallback snapshot
If DefiLlama doesn't answer, the page uses a snapshot baked by bake/bake.mjs and says so at the top.
Code
All computation is in js/model.js; charts use Observable Plot 0.6.17.
