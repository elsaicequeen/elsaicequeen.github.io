A listing desk run by an AI agent, approved by a person

CoinDCX

The agent prepares each token-listing pass. Deterministic checks confirm the exact contract and screen for sell-blocks, freeze or confiscation rights and punitive taxes. A person approves every production write after a dry run, and each write is read back and saved as an audit receipt. Each of four listing incidents became a permanent guard.

Why volume fell

CoinDCX

I split the drop into trader count, orders per trader and order size, reconciled the split to cohort movements and checked it against six other venues on matched days. Most of the fall was traders leaving, not less trading by the ones who stayed.

5.5 h

how far off a peak-hours plan was

Two numbers that looked fine

CoinDCX

Order times were stored in local time but labelled UTC, which had put the growth plan’s peak-activity window 5.5 hours off. I corrected it and wrote down the timestamp basis so the next analysis starts from it.

Separately, the trading fee rose in steps and revenue went up, so the headline said it worked. Split by trader segment, the heaviest 1% of traders made 53–68% of monthly volume, and they had cheaper venues to move to.

I recommended holdout tests before any further fee change.
